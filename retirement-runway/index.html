    Assumptions

    Official CPI has averaged about 5% over the last decade and printed far lower in 2025. It tracks a national basket heavy in food. A retired urban household spends more on health, help, services and travel, which rise faster. Realistic uses 6.5% for essentials, 11% for health, 7% for discretionary and occasional costs, 8% for travel. Stress adds about 1.5% to each.

    Rates used (Oct–Dec 2026): SCSS 8.2%, Post Office MIS 7.4%, PPF 7.1%, RBI Floating Rate Bonds 8.05%, senior-citizen FDs about 7% at large banks and 8–8.5% at small finance banks. Annuity rates are approximate for age 60 and rise slightly with age; get an insurer’s quote before buying.

    Tax: FD, SCSS, POMIS, RBI bonds and annuities are taxed at your slab every year. Debt funds and funds under 35% equity are taxed at slab, but only when you redeem. Equity funds (65%+ equity) pay 12.5% on long-term gains above ₹1.25 L a year per person. Funds with 35–65% equity, which includes many multi-asset funds, pay 12.5% after two years. Gold ETFs pay 12.5% after one year. The tool turns these into an approximate yearly tax drag.

    Ups and downs: equity swings about 17% a year, gold about 15%, bonds 2–3%, with equity and gold moving independently. That is why a multi-asset fund with some gold has smaller falls than a balanced fund with the same return. The mix is rebalanced once a year.

    This is a planning aid, not financial advice. Rates change every quarter. Review it every year with your actual numbers.
