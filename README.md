# India money tools

Two browser-only tools for Indian households. No build step, no server, nothing leaves the device.

- **[Retirement Runway](retirement-runway/)** — how long your savings last at your spending: 1,000-path Monte Carlo, official vs realistic inflation, every investment avenue compared after tax, a three-bucket plan.
- **[Rupee for Life](rupee-for-life/)** — a 22-chapter interactive trainer on every Indian investment option, its taxation under both regimes and every slab, and what it earns after tax at every life stage. Includes a personal-tax and advance-tax calculator, glossary, quizzes and six family cases.

## Hosting

Publish the repository root as it is. On GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main`, folder `/ (root)`. The site then lives at `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/`, with the tools at `/retirement-runway/` and `/rupee-for-life/`.

Rates and rules are as of 1 October–31 December 2026 and change every quarter. For education and planning only; nothing here is investment advice.
